Scope & method

Convoy Stitch does not simulate DCS pathfinding. It makes route complexity visible, prioritizes choke-point testing, and records the first observed break in an actual mission. Thresholds are deliberately conservative user-planning heuristics. Confirm behavior in the current DCS version and preserve a short track or mission when reporting a reproducible defect.
