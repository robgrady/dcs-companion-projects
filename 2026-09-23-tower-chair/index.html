Plain-language glossary

Hold short
Stop before the runway boundary. Do not cross or enter until explicitly cleared.
Line up and wait
Enter the runway and align for departure, but remain stopped until takeoff clearance.
Cleared for takeoff
Authorization to begin the takeoff. Read back the runway and clearance.
Upwind / departure leg
The path flown after takeoff, generally along the extended runway centerline.
Crosswind
The pattern leg perpendicular to the runway after departure.
Downwind
The pattern leg parallel to the runway in the opposite direction of landing.
Base
The leg connecting downwind to final.
Final
The approach path aligned with the landing runway.
Go around
Discontinue the landing, add power, climb, and follow the controller or published pattern instructions.
Number two
You are second in the landing sequence. Identify and follow the aircraft ahead while preserving spacing.
Information Alpha
The named ATIS weather/airport-information edition you have received. The letter changes as information updates.
Readback
Your concise repeat of safety-critical parts of a clearance so the controller can catch errors.
