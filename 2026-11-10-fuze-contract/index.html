Fuze language decoder

Keep separate concepts separate.

REFERENCE
Current DCS core guardrail: since DCS 2.9.30.28536, delayed non-penetrator bombs or rockets used on hard surfaces can shatter instead of detonating. Treat construction, surface, and desired effect as one decision.
Arming delay ≠ function delay

Arming or safe-separation logic prevents a weapon from functioning too close to the aircraft. Function delay controls how long after impact or another trigger the weapon acts. A displayed aircraft value may be a cue rather than the ground-set fuze value; verify the module documentation.

Nose, tail, and paired fuzes

The selected station can change available behavior and redundancy. Do not infer capability from the label alone. Record the exact rearm-panel selection and confirm what the cockpit profile expects.

Penetrator vs standard HE

A purposeful penetrator is designed around surviving impact before functioning. A standard high-explosive body is not automatically made into a penetrator by adding delay.

Why “verified” is manual

DCS implementation differs by weapon, aircraft, campaign, and version. Verification means you checked a current primary source or ran a controlled in-sim test—not that this generic desk certified the setting.
