The support discipline

  1. Contract before launch. Know the support gate, abort condition, and defensive branch before committing.
  2. Preserve illumination. A semi-active radar homing weapon depends on reflected radar energy; an unsupported shot should be treated as lost unless your module documents another behavior.
  3. Crank with awareness. Reduce closure or displacement only while the track remains inside the radar's usable limits.
  4. Do not stare at the target. Keep RWR, fuel, wingman, and the wider picture in the scan.
  5. Survival outranks sunk cost. A defensive break can invalidate your shot; log it honestly and rebuild the fight.

This app teaches decision order, not aircraft-specific switchology. Exact radar limits, indications, missile behavior, and brevity vary by module and server.
