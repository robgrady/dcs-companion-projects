03 / REVIEW

Read timing as evidence.

A segment outside its target is not automatically the cause of a poor pass. Groove Tape shows where timing changed so you can compare it with your own cockpit scan, geometry, energy state, LSO feedback, campaign grade, or Tacview review.

Useful review loop

  1. Hold the aircraft, mission, weather, and personal timing contract stable.
  2. Record at least three passes.
  3. Look for the segment with the widest spread, not merely the largest single miss.
  4. Choose one correction to test next.
  5. Start a new named session when the contract changes.
No automatic flying grade: timing alone cannot establish lineup, glideslope, angle of attack, airspeed, configuration, clearance, or safety.

Field notes

Groove Tape is an offline manual event recorder for DCS World carrier pattern practice. It does not connect to DCS, read telemetry, replace an LSO, or encode a particular aircraft's published procedure.

Keyboard

During an armed pass: 1 Break, 2 Abeam, 3 90, 4 Groove, T Trap, B Bolter, W Waveoff, and Esc Abort. Alt+1 through Alt+4 switch views.

Timing model

The pass clock begins when you mark Break. A pass can be started early and held armed until that instant. Each later mark records elapsed browser monotonic time. The ledger stores segment durations and compares them only with the personal targets active when the pass began.

Privacy

No network requests, remote fonts, analytics, accounts, or external libraries are used. Sessions remain in this browser's local storage unless you export a backup or CSV.

Limits

Browser focus, pauses, device sleep, human button delay, and multitasking can distort timing. Use the tool for repeatable self-review, not real-world aviation, official qualification, or safety decisions.
