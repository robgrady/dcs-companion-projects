Control protocol

Minimum useful discipline
  1. Clone or restart the same test mission so target state is reset.
  2. Hold target type, skill, aspect, weather, attack profile, and range as constant as practical.
  3. Log misses and aborted passes; excluding them can hide delivery inconsistency.
  4. Separate weapon effect from delivery accuracy by recording estimated hits when possible.
  5. Change one variable between saved sets and describe that change in the label.

Damage Ledger does not determine hit location, armor penetration, health, AI logic, fragmentation, or whether a result is a simulator defect. Track files and DCS logs remain the stronger evidence for a reproducible report.
