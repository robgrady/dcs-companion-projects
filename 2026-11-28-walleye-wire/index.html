What this trains

This is a coordination rehearsal, not a switchology emulator. The target is fixed; apparent motion comes from a simulated moving viewpoint, vibration, video noise, and occasional signal loss.

  • Before release: settle the reticle inside the amber capture circle and hold designate long enough to establish a track.
  • After release: keep the crosshair near the aimpoint. Large, frantic reversals reduce smoothness even if the average alignment is acceptable.
  • Weathered: one short video dropout is deliberate. Keep your last correction trend; do not invent a large input while blind.

The loadout gate encodes only the compatibility explicitly added in DCS update 2.9.32.30490: Walleye II on A-6E pylons 2 and 4, with AN/AWW-13 on pylon 3.
