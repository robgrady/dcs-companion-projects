BRAA, not Bullseye

BRAA is Bearing, Range, Altitude, Aspect relative to your aircraft. A Bullseye call is relative to a shared fixed point and cannot be plotted here without Bullseye and ownship coordinates.

Headings are treated as magnetic because DCS AWACS BRAA calls and cockpit headings are normally used that way. This is a geometry aid—not a sensor or tactical authority.
